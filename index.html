(AVAIL) Available = net Accepted at Site stock (already-installed piles deducted) carried into the day, before that day's use. (IN) Inbound = qty arriving that day (Expected Arrival); it rolls into Available the next day. Used = piles the plan installs that day; the balance carries to the next day.
