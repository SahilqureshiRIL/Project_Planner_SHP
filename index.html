Priority & Material-wise Check

Required = piles this plan needs for the material within the selected window (at the chosen machines & productivity). In stock = net Accepted-at-Site stock now. In transit = ordered material arriving within the window. Gap / shortage = period demand not covered by stock + in-transit — these piles can't be installed until more material is ordered. Work halts on = the day that material runs out at this plan's pace.

Color by:

(AVAIL) Available = net Accepted at Site stock (already-installed piles deducted) carried into the day, before that day's use. (IN) Inbound = qty arriving that day (Expected Arrival); it rolls into Available the next day. Used = piles the plan installs that day; the balance carries to the next day. Inbound shows on off days too, since material still arrives.
